## User Manual for Alexander Arévalo

## My role

- I am working on Website Developer
- I am working Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM (El Salvador time) / 1:00 AM to 4:00 AM (Australia time).

## How I work best

- I do my best work when I have good communication with my team and the tasks are clear. Likewise, I work better in an environment where I feel comfortable working, making mistakes, and learning from them. I also like working while listening to music, depending on the time and the task at hand, I find it helps me stay focused.
- I struggle when the task or objective is not sufficiently clear, or there is not enough information to work with. Another cause could be mental fatigue from being overloaded with university activities or projects.

## Communication Style

- I prefer messages to be direct and of the necessary length to cover all the required details.
- The best way to reach me is via email or Discord; I always try to keep an eye on notifications and respond as soon as possible.
- If you need something urgent, you can call me via Discord or schedule an urgent meeting to discuss the situation.

## Collaboration & Teamwork

- I work best in a team when we have good communication, we all support one another, ask all the necessary questions without feeling judged, and offer support to each other.
- My biggest pet peeve in teamwork is poor communication—when team members keep their problems to themselves, fail to ask necessary questions, and do not detail or explain tasks in the best possible way.
- If I seem quiet in a meeting, it usually means I am thinking and processing the situation, as well as developing a possible solution or ideas to contribute to the conversation, I always like to give an idea plenty of thought before speaking.

## Focus & Productivity

- I stay focused by being in a comfortable, uninterrupted environment. I also like listening to music, as I feel it immerses me in the activity and helps me complete it more quickly and efficiently.
- Things that distract me are digital notifications and phone alerts. Also, external things happening at home, or communication with members of my university team.
- The best way to support my productivity is sending as few messages or emails as possible, I prefer one long message or several short ones.

## Feedback & Growth

- I like feedback to be clear, actionable, and focused on specific behaviors rather than general traits. It helps when comments are constructive, timely, and delivered in a supportive way that guides future improvement.
- When I make a mistake, the best way to let me know is to tell me right away, in private and by focusing only on the specific action.
- I appreciate it when teammates communicate clearly, share the workload fairly, mutual support and a positive energy is felt.
