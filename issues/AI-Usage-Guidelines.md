## Use of AI Tools

## Research & Learn

**What AI tools are typically used for your role?**

In the field of web development, Figma AI is often used—for instance, to create a potential page design based on an idea—as is v0 by Vercel, which generates UI components directly from prompts.

**What are the benefits and risks of using AI in a professional setting?**

Using AI in a professional setting offers major benefits like high speed, lower costs, and automation of routine tasks. However, I believe the difference between AI and human work is that people know their limits, whereas AI does not, for instance, if AI is asked to modify a project, such as adding or editing modules, there is a risk that it might publish access keys or even expose the .env file.

**What types of information should never be entered into AI tools?**

We should never enter private personal data, secret work files, medical records, or passwords into AI tools

**How can you fact-check and validate AI-generated content to ensure accuracy?**

To verify and validate AI-generated content, you must compare the data against reliable primary sources, independently test logical calculations using external tools, and always review all generated content.

## Reflection

**When should you use AI for assistance, and when should you rely on your own skills?**

I consider it appropriate to use AI for quick, repetitive tasks such as organizing data, creating initial drafts, and explaining concepts. However, I believe I must rely on my own abilities when making critical decisions or engaging in activities that require personal responsibility and genuine mastery.

**How can you avoid over-reliance on AI while still benefiting from it?**

I believe AI should be viewed and used as a supportive assistant rather than a replacement for your own judgment. This is achieved by setting limits on its use, independently verifying information against reliable sources, and practicing manual or problem-solving skills without relying on AI.

**What steps will you take to ensure data privacy when using AI tools?**

First of all, personal or sensitive data should never be entered into AI, as this is considered a data leak. Similarly, configure privacy settings to prevent conversations from being used to retrain the models.
