## Occupational Health & Safety (OHS) for Desk-Based Work

## Research & Learn

**What are the risks of using a laptop without an external monitor or keyboard?**

- Neck Strain: Looking down at a low laptop screen forces the neck forward, causing muscle tension and pain.
- Poor Posture: Laptops encourage slouching and rounded shoulders because the screen and keyboard cannot be adjusted independently.
- Reduced workflow space: Cramped screen real estate limits multi tasking efficiency compared to large external setups.

**What ergonomic equipment can improve posture when working on a laptop? (e.g., laptop stands, external keyboards, ergonomic chairs)**

- Laptop Stands or Risers: Elevates the screen to eye level so you do not have to look downward.
- External Keyboard and Mouse: Allows you to type and click with your arms in a comfortable position while the laptop screen is elevated.
- Ergonomic Chairs: Provides proper lumbar support to keep your spine aligned during long coding sessions.
- External Monitors: Provides flexible height and depth control for deeper workstation configurations.

**What adjustments should be made to monitor height, chair position, and desk setup for a healthier workspace?**

- Monitor Height: The top of the screen should be at or slightly below eye level.
- Chair Position: Feet flat on the floor, knees bent at a 90 degree angle, and lower back supported by the chair back.
- Desk Setup: Elbows bent at a 90 to 100-degree angle with forearms parallel to the floor while typing.

**What are some daily habits that reduce the impact of prolonged laptop use? (e.g., movement breaks, posture checks, eye strain reduction techniques)**

- Movement Breaks: Stand up, stretch, or walk around every 30 to 60 minutes and stretch your wrists and fingers regularly.
- Posture Checks: Pause periodically to reset the shoulders, relax the jaw, and sit upright. Also, Sit with your feet flat on the floor, keep your knees at a 90 degree angle.
- Eye Strain Reduction: Follow the 20-20-20 rule every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds, blink often to keep your eyes wet and adjust screen brightness to match the room light.
