## What Do You Want to Learn & Contribute?

**What do you hope to learn from this internship?**

At university, we are taught many things. However, I consider them to be the basics that allow us to later specialize in different fields. And one of the things we aren't fully prepared for the actual work environment. And nowadays, almost all companies prioritize experience when hiring. And university cannot provide that kind of experience. That is why this internship is so important to me, being surrounded by experienced people allows me to learn a lot from them, to learn about real world problems and how to solve them. And in this way, gain experience in a real work environment.

**What skills are you most excited to develop?**

In the field of web development, I aim to deepen my knowledge of UI/UX design, page development, various frameworks, and the logic that makes web pages function. I also want to learn more about responsiveness and gain a better understanding of page deployment. Beyond the technical realm, I would like to improve my communication and teamwork skills, enhance my logical thinking for problem-solving, and learn how to adapt to a new environment.

**What do you think you can contribute to the team?**

I believe that every person is unique, with their own distinct way of thinking and perspective on things. I believe I can contribute my unique mindset to problem-solving or proposing solutions. Likewise, I intend to apply all the skills I have acquired, both inside and outside of university. Likewise, I consider myself someone who devotes their full dedication to any project; therefore, I plan to invest all the necessary effort, time, and commitment to help improve the team.

**What is one area you feel less confident in that you’d like to improve?**

Generally, when working on a project, I tend to focus too heavily on the minutiae until everything is nearly perfect; I recognize that I could improve in this area, as it often leads to a loss of team productivity. I also frequently volunteer for more tasks than I can realistically handle, and I would like to learn to communicate better with my team and place greater trust in them. On the technical side, I aim to improve and deepen my knowledge regarding making the website functional and enhancing its performance during user interaction.

**What does success in this internship look like for you?**

I measure the success of this internship by two things: the number of new things I can learn and improve upon, and the number of things I can contribute to the team to make it better. I would like to gain the necessary experience to excel in the professional world, and to acquire technical knowledge that will make me a better developer, engineer, and person.
