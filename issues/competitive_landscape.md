## Research Assignment: Competitive Landscape

**What makes Focus Bear different from these apps?**

Freedom and Cold Turkey offer only features for blocking specific websites or apps to help maintain focus and avoid distractions. Tiimo and Routinery focus solely on planning daily routines—scheduling activities, allocating time for each, and setting alarms to start them. Focus Bear combines both capabilities: it blocks websites and apps to prevent distractions while working and includes a section for planning and customizing routines.

**If you were a user, why would you choose Focus Bear over competitors?**

With Focus Bear, I find in a single place what I would otherwise need two other apps to get. In other words, each of the other apps mentioned only does half of what Focus Bear offers—whether it’s preventing distractions or planning routines, but not both. I would choose Focus Bear because I see no need to download two apps when there is a single app that combines the functions of both. Furthermore, it is the only one designed with a focus on people with ADHD and ASD.

**What’s one feature that other apps have that Focus Bear doesn’t?**

- Freedom has a cross-platform session synchronization that initiates an activity block across all devices from a single location, featuring a "locked mode" that prevents the session from being ended prematurely once started.
- Cold Turkey features an extreme Frozen Turkey mode capable of completely blocking access to the entire computer screen or device, this is a deep, system-level lock that prevents access to local files and folders.
- Tiimo features a visual, color-coded timeline utilizing a comprehensive library of custom icons which maps out the entire day graphically to help users intuitively perceive the passage of time.
- Routinery features a location-based GPS triggers that automatically start a specific routine when someone arrives at a designated place.

**Based on your research, what’s one improvement you think Focus Bear could make?**

It would likely be beneficial to incorporate a design based on colors or icons/images and include a "timeline" of the entire day, similar to Tiimo, as well as a GPS feature like the one in Routinery, to keep track of activities that need to be done away from home. And perhaps add another, slightly stricter mode, like the one Cold Turkey has.
