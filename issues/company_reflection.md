## Focus Bear: Mission and Vision

**Why do you think Focus Bear was created?**

Focus Bear was created to actually solve a problem that involves people with ADHD. As the founder and CEO said, there are a lot of systems or apps that try to solve a problem, but they only think superficially. For example, I believe that people can try and help you, but if they haven't gone through the same thing as me, the help isn't the same compared to that from someone who has actually gone through the same thing or has the same problem as me. So I think that Focus Bear was created by and for people with ADHD, so they actually feel identified by it and feel they are receiving genuine and productive help.

**What problem is Focus Bear solving?**

Focus Bear is trying to help people with ADHD and ASD to be more productive and keep their thoughts in order, reducing decision fatigue, increase focus when doing daily activities and feel more motivated. Focus try to solve problems with 5 main activities, that are Reducing distractions, Help to start the day right, Eliminate decision fatigue, Use visual cues to increase focus and Increase motivation.

**Why do you think this mission is important?**

As the website said, reduced productivity due to ADHD costs money, but, for me, it's no the important part. ADHD and ASD affects everyone, regardless of age. So, in children, they can find studying and making new relationships with other children, harder than usual, and often, they don't receive the necessary attention, and the problem persists or even worsens as the years go by. For teenagers and adults, it affects their day-to-day activities, and they are likely less productive than they would like to be, even though it is not their fault. So those productivity issues are reflected in university, at work, in relationships, with friends, and in their personal life.

**How does Focus Bear’s work align with your personal values or interests?**

I've met people with these conditions in school, and I remember that they found it more difficult than the rest to study or pay attention in class, and as a kid, I didn't really understand the reasons until I grew up. So, looking back now, I would have liked to help them in a different and better way. And just like those who were in my circle of acquaintances, there are millions of people in the world just like them, and if I can help or contribute to assit them, It would be perfect for my interests, because it’s a win-win situation, I learn from the experience and can help contribute.

**Do you personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve?**

I have never been diagnosed by a professional, but I definitely find some days harder than others when it comes to daily activities, this affects my university studies, my freelance work, and the activities I pursue outside of my field.
